# Hospital Readmission Risk Analysis


This project analyzes hospital patient data to identify factors associated with 30-day readmissions.
The goal is to uncover patterns that can help healthcare providers reduce readmission rates and improve patient outcomes.


# Key Insights:

*  Patients with 6+ comorbidities show the highest readmission rates.
* Older patients (65+) are significantly more likely to be readmitted.
* Discharge to skilled nursing facilities is strongly associated with higher readmission risk.
* Longer hospital stays (8+ days) increase likelihood of readmission.

  

# Business Recommendations
*  Implement targeted care plans for patients with 6+ comorbidities, as they show the highest readmission rates
* Provide closer monitoring and follow-up appointments after discharge
* Flag long-stay patients as high-risk before discharge
* Provide continuous care plans instead of one-time treatment
* Provide follow-up within 48–72 hours after discharge
